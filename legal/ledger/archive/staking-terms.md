# NIDAW Staking Terms

**Last Updated:** [Date]

## 1. Staking Overview
NIDAW staking allows you to lock your NIDAW tokens for a specified period in exchange for rewards. Staking supports network security and platform growth.

## 2. Eligibility
To stake NIDAW tokens, you must:
- Hold NIDAW tokens in a compatible wallet.
- Complete KYC/AML verification (for custodial staking).
- Agree to these Terms.
- Not be in a restricted jurisdiction.

## 3. Staking Parameters
### 3.1 Lock Periods
| Period | APY | Minimum Stake |
|--------|-----|---------------|
| 30 days | 5% | 100 NIDAW |
| 60 days | 8% | 100 NIDAW |
| 90 days | 10% | 500 NIDAW |
| 180 days | 12% | 1,000 NIDAW |
| 365 days | 15% | 5,000 NIDAW |

### 3.2 Reward Distribution
- Rewards paid monthly in NIDAW tokens.
- Rewards calculated daily, distributed on the 1st of each month.
- Rewards subject to 30-day vesting period.

## 4. Staking Process
### 4.1 How to Stake
1. Connect your wallet to the NIDAW platform.
2. Select lock period and amount.
3. Approve the staking transaction.
4. Tokens are locked in the smart contract.

### 4.2 Unstaking
- Tokens can be unstaked after the lock period ends.
- Early unstaking incurs a 50% penalty on accrued rewards.
- Unstaking takes 24-48 hours to process.

## 5. Rewards Calculation
Rewards are calculated as:

Reward = (Staked Amount × APY × Days Staked) / 365


Example:
- Staked: 10,000 NIDAW
- APY: 10%
- Period: 90 days
- Reward = (10,000 × 0.10 × 90) / 365 = 246.58 NIDAW

## 6. Risks
### 6.1 Smart Contract Risk
Staking involves smart contract vulnerabilities. While audited, no system is 100% secure.

### 6.2 Token Price Risk
Token value may fluctuate. Rewards are paid in tokens, not fiat.

### 6.3 Liquidity Risk
Tokens are locked and cannot be traded or used during the lock period.

### 6.4 Regulatory Risk
Staking may be subject to future regulations.

## 7. Slashing (Future)
In future versions, slashing may apply for:
- Validator misbehavior (if NIDAW becomes a PoS network).
- Protocol violations.

Slashing penalties: 1-10% of staked amount.

## 8. Tax Implications
Staking rewards may be taxable as income. Consult a tax professional.

## 9. Termination
NIDAW may terminate the staking program with 30 days notice. Locked tokens will be returned.

## 10. Limitation of Liability
NIDAW is not liable for:
- Smart contract exploits.
- Token price changes.
- Regulatory actions.
- Lost rewards due to user error.
