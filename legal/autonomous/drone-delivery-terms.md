# NIDAW Drone Delivery Terms

**Last Updated:** [Date]

## 1. Service Overview
NIDAW Drone Delivery uses autonomous drones to deliver food, packages, and goods. This service is experimental and subject to regulatory approval.

## 2. Eligibility
To use drone delivery, you must:
- Be in a service area with drone delivery availability.
- Provide a safe, legal, and accessible drop-off zone.
- Comply with local drone regulations.
- Accept these Terms.

## 3. Delivery Process
### 3.1 Ordering
- Place an order via the NIDAW app.
- Select "Drone Delivery" at checkout (if available).
- Provide precise drop-off coordinates.

### 3.2 Delivery
- Drone departs from the restaurant/warehouse.
- Real-time tracking available in the app.
- Drone lands or hovers at the drop-off zone.
- Package is released via winch or landing.

### 3.3 Confirmation
- You receive a notification upon delivery.
- Photos of the delivery are available in the app.

## 4. User Responsibilities
### 4.1 Drop-Off Zone
You must provide:
- A clear, flat, and unobstructed area (minimum 3m x 3m).
- Free from overhead obstacles (trees, wires, buildings).
- Legal for drone landing (check local regulations).
- Safe for people and pets (keep clear during delivery).

### 4.2 Safety
- Do not approach the drone during delivery.
- Keep children and pets away.
- Do not attempt to touch or interfere with the drone.
- Follow all in-app safety instructions.

## 5. Limitations
### 5.1 Weather
Drone delivery may be suspended due to:
- High winds (>25 mph).
- Rain, snow, or fog.
- Extreme temperatures.
- Poor visibility.

### 5.2 Airspace
Delivery may be delayed or cancelled due to:
- Airspace restrictions (airports, military zones).
- Temporary flight restrictions (events, emergencies).
- Regulatory changes.

### 5.3 Payload
Drone delivery is limited to:
- Weight: Up to 5 kg.
- Size: Up to 40cm x 40cm x 40cm.
- No hazardous materials, liquids, or perishables (unless specified).

## 6. Liability
### 6.1 NIDAW Liability
NIDAW is liable for:
- Damage caused by drone malfunction (excluding user error).
- Lost or damaged packages (up to declared value).
- Delays due to NIDAW system failures.

### 6.2 User Liability
You are liable for:
- Damage caused by unsafe drop-off zones.
- Injuries from interfering with the drone.
- Violation of local drone regulations.

## 7. Privacy
Drones are equipped with cameras for navigation and safety. Footage is:
- Encrypted and stored securely.
- Deleted after 30 days (unless required for incident investigation).
- Not shared with third parties (except law enforcement).

## 8. Regulatory Compliance
NIDAW complies with:
- FAA Part 107 (US).
- EASA regulations (EU).
- Local drone laws.

Operations may be suspended without notice due to regulatory changes.

## 9. Termination
NIDAW may terminate drone delivery in your area with 30 days notice.
