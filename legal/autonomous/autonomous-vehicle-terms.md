# NIDAW Autonomous Vehicle (AV) & Drone Delivery Terms

## 1. Experimental Nature of Service
NIDAW’s Autonomous Vehicle (Robotaxi) and Drone Delivery services utilize advanced, experimental AI and robotics technology. While rigorously tested, these systems may behave unpredictably in complex, real-world environments.

## 2. User Responsibilities (AV)
- **Stay Alert:** You must remain awake, attentive, and ready to take manual control (if applicable) or exit the vehicle safely in an emergency.
- **Follow Instructions:** You must obey all in-vehicle prompts, voice commands, and safety guidelines.
- **Prohibited Actions:** Do not interfere with sensors, cameras, or steering mechanisms. Do not bring hazardous materials or unauthorized passengers.

## 3. Limitation of Liability for Autonomous Operations
NIDAW shall not be liable for minor delays, route inefficiencies, or non-injurious disruptions caused by autonomous system limitations (e.g., weather-induced sensor degradation, map inaccuracies). 

## 4. Drone Delivery Specifics

- NIDAW is not responsible for packages damaged by extreme weather, wildlife, or unauthorized third-party interference after the drone has initiated the final drop sequence.
- Drone operations are strictly subject to local aviation authority regulations (e.g., FAA, EASA) and may be suspended without notice due to airspace restrictions.

## 5. Indemnification
You agree to indemnify NIDAW against any claims arising from your misuse of autonomous features, failure to follow safety protocols, or provision of inaccurate drop-off coordinates.
