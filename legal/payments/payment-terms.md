# NIDAW Payment Terms

## 1. Authorized Payment Methods
NIDAW accepts major credit/debit cards, digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), and NIDAW Tokens (where applicable). By adding a payment method, you represent that you are authorized to use it.

## 2. Authorization and Charging
- **Pre-authorization:** For rides and deliveries, we may place a temporary hold on your payment method to ensure funds are available. This is not a charge and will be released within 3-5 business days.
- **Final Charge:** You will be charged the final amount upon completion of the service, which may differ from the estimate due to route changes, tolls, or waiting time.

## 3. Dynamic Pricing & Surcharges
Prices are calculated using AI-driven dynamic pricing. During periods of high demand, bad weather, or heavy traffic, a "Surge Multiplier" may apply. You will always be shown the estimated fare and must explicitly accept it before confirming the service.

## 4. Tipping
Tips are 100% passed to the driver or delivery personnel. Tips are final and non-refundable once the service is completed.

## 5. Disputed Charges
If you believe a charge is incorrect, you must notify NIDAW Support within 30 days of the transaction. We will investigate and, if an error is confirmed, issue a refund or credit. Unauthorized chargebacks may result in immediate account suspension.
