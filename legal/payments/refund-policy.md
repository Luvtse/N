# NIDAW Refund Policy

**Last Updated:** [Date]

## 1. Refund Eligibility
You may be eligible for a refund if:
- The service was not provided (e.g., driver no-show, restaurant closed).
- Significant service failure (e.g., wrong order, major delay).
- Duplicate charge or billing error.
- Cancellation per our cancellation policy.
- Unauthorized transaction (fraud).

## 2. Refund Process
### 2.1 How to Request
- **In-App:** Go to Help > Report an Issue > Request Refund.
- **Email:** refunds@nidaw.com with transaction ID and details.
- **Phone:** +1-800-NIDAW-HELP (24/7).

### 2.2 Timeline
- Refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction.
- We investigate and respond within 5 business days.
- Approved refunds are processed within 5-10 business days.

### 2.3 Refund Method
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- For credit cards: 5-10 business days (depending on your bank).
- For digital wallets: 1-3 business days.
- For NIDAW tokens: Immediate (on-chain).

## 3. Service-Specific Policies

### 3.1 Rides
- **Driver no-show:** Full refund.
- **Cancellation within 2 minutes:** Full refund.
- **Cancellation after 2 minutes:** 50% refund (varies by region).
- **Route deviation:** Partial refund (pro-rated).
- **Overcharge:** Full refund of difference.

### 3.2 Hotels
- Per hotel cancellation policy (displayed at booking).
- **Free cancellation:** Full refund if cancelled within window.
- **Non-refundable:** No refund (clearly marked).
- **Early checkout:** Partial refund (at hotel discretion).

### 3.3 Food Delivery
- **Wrong/missing items:** Refund for affected items.
- **Cold/damaged food:** Full refund.
- **Late delivery (>30 min):** Delivery fee refund.
- **Restaurant error:** Full refund.

### 3.4 Freight
- Per carrier terms.
- **Damage/loss:** Claim via insurance (up to declared value).
- **Delay:** Partial refund (pro-rated).

## 4. Non-Refundable Items
The following are generally non-refundable:
- Cancellation fees (per policy).
- No-show fees.
- Tips (once service is completed).
- Expired credits or promotions.
- Custom/cancelled services.

## 5. Disputed Charges
If you believe a charge is unauthorized:
- Contact us immediately at fraud@nidaw.com.
- We will investigate and temporarily credit your account.
- If confirmed fraudulent, permanent refund issued.
- False fraud claims may result in account suspension.

## 6. Chargebacks
If you initiate a chargeback without contacting us first:
- Your account may be suspended pending investigation.
- Repeated chargebacks may result in permanent termination.
- We reserve the right to dispute fraudulent chargebacks.

## 7. Credits vs. Refunds
In some cases, we may offer NIDAW credits instead of refunds:
- Credits are valid for 12 months.
- Can be used for any NIDAW service.
- Non-transferable and non-refundable.

## 8. Contact
For refund inquiries:
- **Email:** refunds@nidaw.com
- **Phone:** +1-800-NIDAW-HELP
- **In-App:** Help > Report an Issue
