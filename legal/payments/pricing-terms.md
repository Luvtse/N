# NIDAW Pricing Terms

## 1. Dynamic Pricing Model
NIDAW uses AI-driven dynamic pricing to optimize supply and demand. Prices may fluctuate based on:
- **Demand:** Number of active riders/orders in the area.
- **Supply:** Number of available drivers/providers.
- **Time:** Rush hours, late nights, weekends, holidays.
- **Weather:** Rain, snow, extreme temperatures.
- **Traffic:** Congestion, road closures, accidents.
- **Events:** Concerts, sports games, conferences.

## 2. Fare Components

### 2.1 Rides
- **Base Fare:** Fixed fee per trip (varies by ride type).
- **Time Rate:** Per-minute charge during the trip.
- **Distance Rate:** Per-kilometer/mile charge.
- **Surge Multiplier:** Applied during high demand (1.0x - 3.5x).
- **Booking Fee:** Platform fee (displayed upfront).
- **Tolls:** Passed through at cost.
- **Waiting Time:** Charged after 5 minutes of driver arrival.

### 2.2 Hotels
- **Room Rate:** Per-night charge (displayed upfront).
- **Taxes:** Local taxes (included or displayed separately).
- **Resort Fees:** If applicable (disclosed before booking).
- **Service Fee:** NIDAW booking fee (10-15%).

### 2.3 Food Delivery
- **Food Cost:** Menu prices.
- **Delivery Fee:** Based on distance and demand.
- **Service Fee:** Platform fee (10-15%).
- **Small Order Fee:** For orders below minimum.
- **Surge Fee:** During peak hours.

### 2.4 Freight
- **Base Rate:** Per shipment.
- **Weight/Volume:** Based on cargo size.
- **Distance:** Origin to destination.
- **Fuel Surcharge:** Variable based on fuel costs.
- **Insurance:** Optional coverage.
- **Customs Fees:** For international shipments.

## 3. Price Estimates
- Estimates are provided before booking.
- Final price may differ due to route changes, tolls, waiting time.
- You must accept the estimated price before confirming.
- Significant deviations (>20%) require re-confirmation.

## 4. Surge Pricing
- Surge multipliers are displayed before booking.
- You can wait for surge to decrease (no obligation to book).
- Surge is capped at 3.5x (varies by region).
- Surge revenue is shared with drivers/providers.

## 5. Price Changes
- NIDAW may change pricing at any time.
- 30 days notice for material changes (where possible).
- Existing bookings honored at original price.
- Continued use constitutes acceptance of new pricing.

## 6. Discounts and Promotions
- Promo codes and discounts are subject to terms.
- Cannot be combined unless explicitly stated.
- May have expiration dates and usage limits.
- NIDAW reserves the right to modify or cancel promotions.

## 7. Currency and Conversion
- Prices displayed in your local currency.
- Exchange rates updated daily.
- Foreign transaction fees may apply (check with your bank).
- NIDAW tokens (NIDAW) accepted where applicable.

## 8. Disputes
If you believe a price is incorrect:
- Contact support within 7 days of the transaction.
- Provide transaction ID and details.
- We will investigate and adjust if an error is confirmed.
