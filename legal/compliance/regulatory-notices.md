# NIDAW Regulatory Notices

## 1. Transportation Regulations
NIDAW complies with local transportation regulations in all operating jurisdictions. This includes:
- Driver licensing and background checks.
- Vehicle safety inspections.
- Insurance requirements.
- Fare regulations.

## 2. Food Safety
NIDAW complies with food safety regulations (FDA, USDA, local health departments). Restaurant partners must:
- Hold valid food service licenses.
- Follow hygiene protocols.
- Maintain proper food storage temperatures.

## 3. Lodging Regulations
NIDAW complies with lodging regulations (hotel licensing, occupancy taxes, safety codes). Hotel partners must:
- Hold valid lodging licenses.
- Comply with fire safety codes.
- Pay applicable taxes.

## 4. Drone Regulations
NIDAW complies with drone regulations (FAA Part 107, EASA). Operations include:
- Pilot certification.
- Airspace authorization.
- Safety protocols.

## 5. Autonomous Vehicle Regulations
NIDAW complies with autonomous vehicle regulations (NHTSA, state laws). Operations include:
- Safety testing.
- Reporting requirements.
- Insurance coverage.

## 6. Data Privacy
NIDAW complies with:
- GDPR (EU/EEA).
- CCPA/CPRA (California).
- LGPD (Brazil).
- Other local privacy laws.

## 7. Financial Regulations
NIDAW complies with:
- PCI DSS (payment security).
- AML/KYC (anti-money laundering).
- Tax reporting.
