# NIDAW Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

To ensure a safe and reliable ecosystem, NIDAW prohibits the following activities. Violations may result in immediate account termination and legal action.

## 1. Prohibited Conduct
- **Illegal Activities:** Using the platform to facilitate or transport illegal substances, stolen goods, or contraband.
- **Harassment & Discrimination:** Abusive, threatening, or discriminatory behavior toward drivers, delivery personnel, support staff, or other users based on race, gender, religion, orientation, or disability.
- **Fraud & Abuse:** Creating fake accounts, exploiting referral bonuses, manipulating GPS location, or using stolen payment methods.
- **Platform Interference:** Attempting to scrape data, reverse-engineer the app, deploy bots, or launch DDoS attacks against NIDAW infrastructure.

## 2. Content Restrictions
Users may not upload or share content that is obscene, defamatory, infringes on intellectual property, or violates privacy rights.

## 3. Enforcement
NIDAW reserves the right to investigate violations, suspend accounts pending investigation, and cooperate with law enforcement. We may use AI-driven monitoring to detect anomalous or prohibited behavior patterns.
