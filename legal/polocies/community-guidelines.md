# NIDAW Community Guidelines

## 1. Our Community
NIDAW is a global community of riders, drivers, delivery personnel, and partners. We strive to create a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment for all.

## 2. Core Values
- **Respect:** Treat everyone with dignity and kindness.
- **Safety:** Prioritize physical and emotional safety.
- **Inclusivity:** Welcome people of all backgrounds.
- **Integrity:** Act honestly and transparently.
- **Accountability:** Take responsibility for your actions.

## 3. Expected Behavior
### 3.1 Riders
- Be punctual and ready at the pickup location.
- Treat drivers with respect and courtesy.
- Keep the vehicle clean.
- Pay promptly and tip fairly.
- Provide honest feedback.

### 3.2 Drivers
- Provide safe, professional service.
- Maintain a clean, comfortable vehicle.
- Communicate clearly with riders.
- Follow traffic laws.
- Respect rider privacy.

### 3.3 Delivery Personnel
- Deliver orders promptly and accurately.
- Handle food/packages with care.
- Communicate delays or issues.
- Follow hygiene protocols.

## 4. Prohibited Behavior
The following are strictly prohibited:
- **Harassment:** Bullying, threats, or abusive language.
- **Discrimination:** Based on race, gender, religion, orientation, disability, etc.
- **Violence:** Physical altercations or threats.
- **Fraud:** Fake accounts, stolen payment methods, manipulation.
- **Illegal Activity:** Drugs, weapons, contraband.
- **Privacy Violations:** Sharing personal information without consent.
- **Property Damage:** Vandalism or destruction.

## 5. Reporting
If you witness or experience prohibited behavior:
- **In-App:** Use the "Report" button.
- **Email:** safety@nidaw.com
- **Phone:** +1-800-NIDAW-SAFE (24/7)
- **Emergency:** Call local authorities first, then NIDAW.

## 6. Enforcement
Violations may result in:
- Warning.
- Temporary suspension.
- Permanent deactivation.
- Legal action.
- Reporting to law enforcement.

## 7. Appeals
If you believe you were unfairly penalized:
- Submit an appeal within 30 days.
- Provide evidence and context.
- Our team will review and respond within 7 days.

## 8. Updates
These guidelines may be updated. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
