# NIDAW Content Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy governs all content uploaded, shared, or submitted on the NIDAW platform, including:
- User profiles (photos, bios).
- Reviews and ratings.
- Messages and communications.
- Uploaded documents (receipts, IDs).

## 2. Acceptable Content
You may share content that is:
- Relevant to NIDAW services.
- Respectful and professional.
- Accurate and truthful.
- Legally compliant.

## 3. Prohibited Content
You may NOT share:
- **Hate Speech:** Content that promotes violence or discrimination.
- **Harassment:** Bullying, threats, or abusive language.
- **Obscenity:** Pornographic, sexually explicit, or gratuitously violent content.
- **Misinformation:** False or misleading information.
- **Spam:** Unsolicited promotions or repetitive content.
- **Copyright Infringement:** Content you don't own or have permission to use.
- **Privacy Violations:** Personal information of others without consent.
- **Illegal Content:** Content that violates laws (drugs, weapons, etc.).

## 4. Moderation
NIDAW uses AI and human moderators to review content. Prohibited content will be:
- Removed.
- Reported to authorities (if illegal).
- Result in account suspension or termination.

## 5. Reporting
To report prohibited content:
- **In-App:** Use the "Report" button.
- **Email:** content@nidaw.com

## 6. Appeals
If your content was removed in error:
- Submit an appeal within 30 days.
- Provide context and justification.
- Our team will review within 7 days.
