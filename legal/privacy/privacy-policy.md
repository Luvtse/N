# NIDAW Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [Date]  

## 1. Introduction
NIDAW Inc. ("NIDAW," "we," "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-native mobility super-app.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Personal Information:** Name, email, phone number, payment details, and government ID (for drivers).
- **Location Data:** Precise real-time GPS data when the app is in use, and approximate location data in the background to optimize AI matching and demand forecasting.
- **Telematics & Device Data:** Device type, OS, IP address, and (for drivers) driving behavior metrics (speed, braking, phone usage).
- **AI & Interaction Data:** Service preferences, search history, and feedback to train our federated learning models and improve personalization.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- To provide, maintain, and improve our Services (e.g., matching riders with drivers, optimizing delivery routes).
- To process payments and prevent fraud.
- To train our AI/ML models using **Federated Learning** (your raw personal data remains on your device; only aggregated, anonymized model weights are shared).
- To comply with legal obligations and ensure safety.

## 4. Information Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:
- **Service Providers:** Drivers (pickup/dropoff info), restaurants, hotels, and payment processors (Stripe, Adyen) to fulfill your requests.
- **Legal Authorities:** When required by law, subpoena, or to prevent imminent harm.
- **Corporate Clients:** If you use NIDAW Corpus, trip data may be shared with your employer for expense management, as permitted by you.

## 5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR/CCPA)
Depending on your location, you have the right to:
- Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
- Opt-out of the "sale" or sharing of personal data (CCPA).
- Withdraw consent for location tracking (though this may limit Service functionality).
- Request data portability.  
To exercise these rights, email privacy@nidaw.com.

## 6. Data Retention & Security
We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined herein, or as required by law (e.g., 7 years for financial records). We employ industry-standard encryption (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit) and regular security audits.

## 7. Children’s Privacy
Our Services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
