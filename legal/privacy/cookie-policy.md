## 1. What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website or use our app. They help us remember your preferences, improve functionality, and provide analytics.

We also use similar technologies:
- **Web Beacons:** Tiny graphics to track user behavior.
- **Local Storage:** Browser-based storage (localStorage, sessionStorage).
- **Device Fingerprinting:** Identifying devices based on characteristics.

## 3. How to Manage Cookies

### 3.1 Cookie Consent Banner
When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner where you can:
- **Accept All:** Allow all cookies.
- **Reject Non-Essential:** Allow only essential cookies.
- **Customize:** Choose specific cookie categories.

### 3.2 Browser Settings
You can control cookies through your browser:

**Chrome:** Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies  
**Firefox:** Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data  
**Safari:** Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and Website Data  
**Edge:** Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions

### 3.3 Opt-Out Links
- **Google Analytics:** https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
- **Facebook:** https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/psettings/guest-controls
- **Your Online Choices (EU):** http://www.youronlinechoices.com
- **Network Advertising Initiative (US):** http://www.networkadvertising.org

### 3.4 Do Not Track
Some browsers support "Do Not Track" (DNT). We currently do not respond to DNT signals but honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) where required.

## 4. Impact of Disabling Cookies
If you disable cookies:
- **Essential cookies:** Website/app may not function properly.
- **Functional cookies:** Preferences won't be saved.
- **Analytics cookies:** We can't improve based on your usage.
- **Marketing cookies:** You'll still see ads, but less relevant.
