## 2. Legal Basis for Processing
We process your personal data based on:
- **Consent:** For marketing, optional features, location tracking.
- **Contract Performance:** To provide services you request.
- **Legal Obligation:** To comply with tax, regulatory, and safety laws.
- **Legitimate Interests:** For security, fraud prevention, service improvement (balanced against your rights).

## 3. Your GDPR Rights
You have the right to:
- **Access:** Obtain a copy of your personal data.
- **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request deletion of your data.
- **Restriction:** Limit processing in certain circumstances.
- **Data Portability:** Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Object:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.
- **Withdraw Consent:** At any time, without affecting prior processing.
- **Lodge a Complaint:** With your local supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@nidaw.com or use the in-app Privacy Center.

## 4. Automated Decision-Making
NIDAW uses AI/ML for:
- Dynamic pricing (surge multipliers).
- Driver-rider matching.
- Fraud detection.

You have the right to:
- Obtain human intervention.
- Express your point of view.
- Contest the decision.

Contact ai-rights@nidaw.com for automated decision appeals.
