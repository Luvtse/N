## 3. Travel Policies and Compliance
### 3.1 Policy Configuration
Client may configure travel policies including:
- Maximum fare/booking limits by service type.
- Allowed ride types, hotel star ratings, restaurant categories.
- Time-based restrictions (e.g., no rides after 10 PM).
- Approval workflows for bookings exceeding thresholds.
- Blocked vendors or locations.

### 3.2 Policy Enforcement
- NIDAW enforces policies at the point of booking.
- Policy violations trigger approval workflows or block bookings.
- Client may override policies for specific employees or situations.

### 3.3 Compliance Reporting
NIDAW provides real-time dashboards showing:
- Policy compliance rates.
- Violation details and trends.
- Employee spending by category.
- Cost savings from policy enforcement.

## 4. Booking and Expense Management
### 4.1 Booking Process
- Employees book services (rides, hotels, food, freight) through the NIDAW app or web portal.
- Bookings are automatically tagged with employee ID, department, and project code.
- Corporate policies are applied in real-time.

### 4.2 Expense Tracking
- All transactions are automatically categorized and reconciled.
- Receipts are captured via OCR (photo upload) or digital receipts.
- Expenses are synced to Client's ERP/accounting system (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, QuickBooks).

### 4.3 Approval Workflows
- Multi-level approval workflows for high-value bookings.
- Manager approval for policy violations.
- Automated approval for bookings within policy limits.
