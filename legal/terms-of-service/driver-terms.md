# NIDAW Driver Terms of Service

**Last Updated:** [Date]  
**Effective Date:** [Date]

## 1. Independent Contractor Status
You are an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or partner of NIDAW Inc. ("NIDAW"). This means:
- You control when, where, and how long you provide services.
- You provide your own vehicle, equipment, and insurance.
- You are responsible for your own taxes, benefits, and business expenses.
- You may provide services to other platforms simultaneously.
- NIDAW does not guarantee minimum earnings or trip volume.

## 2. Eligibility Requirements
To drive with NIDAW, you must:
- Be at least 21 years old (or the legal driving age in your jurisdiction).
- Hold a valid driver's license with at least 1 year of driving experience.
- Pass a comprehensive background check (criminal, driving record, sex offender registry).
- Maintain valid auto insurance meeting local minimum requirements.
- Own or lease a vehicle that meets NIDAW's vehicle standards (age, condition, safety features).
- Pass a vehicle inspection conducted by NIDAW or an authorized partner.
- Possess a smartphone capable of running the NIDAW Driver app.
- Have the legal right to work in your jurisdiction.

## 3. Vehicle Standards
Your vehicle must:
- Be 10 years old or newer (varies by city and service tier).
- Have 4 doors and seat at least 4 passengers comfortably.
- Be in excellent mechanical and cosmetic condition.
- Pass NIDAW's annual vehicle inspection.
- Display valid registration and inspection stickers.
- Meet local emissions and environmental standards.
- For premium/luxury tiers: meet specific make/model requirements.
- For electric/hybrid tiers: have functional charging capabilities.

## 4. Accepting and Completing Trips
When you receive a trip request:
- You have 15 seconds to accept or decline.
- Trip details include pickup location, estimated fare, and (optionally) destination.
- You are not required to accept any trip, but excessive declinations may affect your ability to receive requests.
- When you accept a trip, you agree to:
  - Navigate to the pickup location promptly and safely.
  - Contact the rider if needed to clarify pickup details.
  - Provide safe, professional, and courteous service.
  - Follow the route specified by the rider (or the optimal route if not specified).
  - Complete the trip at the designated drop-off location.
  - Maintain a clean, comfortable, and odor-free vehicle.

## 5. Cancellations
- You may cancel a trip if the rider does not appear within 5 minutes of your arrival.
- Excessive cancellations may result in penalties, reduced request volume, or deactivation.
- If a rider cancels after you are en route, you will receive a cancellation fee.

## 6. Earnings and Payments
### 6.1 Fare Calculation
Your earnings are calculated based on:
- Base fare (fixed per trip).
- Time rate (per minute of active trip time).
- Distance rate (per kilometer/mile traveled).
- Surge pricing (during high-demand periods).
- Tips (100% of tips go to you).
- Bonuses and incentives (when offered by NIDAW).

### 6.2 Payment Schedule
- Earnings are processed weekly (every Monday).
- Minimum payout threshold: $10 USD (or local equivalent).
- Payment methods: Direct deposit, debit card, instant pay (subject to fees).
- Earnings include trip fares, tips, and bonuses.
- Deductions include: NIDAW commission, tolls, fees, and applicable taxes.

### 6.3 Commission and Fees
- NIDAW commission: 20% of fare (varies by service type and region).
- Booking fees: Passed through to the rider, not deducted from your earnings.
- Cancellation fees: You receive 100% of rider cancellation fees.
- Tips: You receive 100% of tips.

### 6.4 Taxes
You are solely responsible for:
- Reporting all income to tax authorities.
- Paying income tax, self-employment tax, and other applicable taxes.
- Maintaining accurate records of earnings and expenses.
- Consulting a tax professional for guidance.

NIDAW will provide you with tax documents (e.g., 1099 in the US) as required by law.

## 7. Ratings and Performance Standards
### 7.1 Rating System
After each trip, riders rate your service on a 5-star scale. Your overall rating is the average of your last 500 trips.

### 7.2 Rating Factors
Riders consider:
- Professionalism and courtesy.
- Vehicle cleanliness and comfort.
- Driving safety and smoothness.
- Navigation accuracy and efficiency.
- Communication and responsiveness.

### 7.3 Performance Standards
To remain active on the platform, you must maintain:
- Minimum rating: 4.6 stars.
- Acceptance rate: 70% (where applicable).
- Cancellation rate: Below 5%.
- Response time: Under 30 seconds.

### 7.4 Performance Improvement
If your performance falls below standards:
- You will receive a warning and improvement plan.
- You may be temporarily deactivated.
- You must complete training or coaching.
- Repeated violations may result in permanent deactivation.

## 8. Safety and Conduct
### 8.1 Professional Conduct
You agree to:
- Treat all riders with respect and courtesy.
- Maintain a clean, odor-free vehicle.
- Dress appropriately and professionally.
- Not discriminate based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
- Not engage in inappropriate conversation or behavior.
- Follow all traffic laws and regulations.

### 8.2 Prohibited Activities
You may NOT:
- Drive under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or impairing medications.
- Use your phone while driving (except for navigation and the NIDAW app).
- Smoke, vape, or consume food/beverages in the vehicle.
- Allow unauthorized passengers or drivers.
- Transport illegal substances, weapons, or hazardous materials.
- Engage in harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior.
- Request off-platform payments or tips.
- Share rider personal information with third parties.
- Engage in fraudulent activity or manipulation.

### 8.3 Safety Features
You agree to use NIDAW safety features:
- Real-time GPS tracking.
- Emergency assistance button.
- RideCheck (automatic crash detection).
- Audio recording (where permitted by law).
- Share trip status with trusted contacts.

### 8.4 Incident Reporting
You must immediately report:
- Accidents or collisions.
- Safety incidents or threats.
- Rider misconduct or abuse.
- Vehicle damage or mechanical issues.
- Lost items.
- Fraudulent activity or suspicious behavior.

### 9.3 Insurance Requirements
You must maintain:

## 11. Deactivation and Appeals
### 11.1 Grounds for Deactivation
We may deactivate your account for:
- Rating below 4.6 stars (after warning period).
- Acceptance rate below 70%.
- Excessive cancellations.
- Safety violations.
- Fraudulent activity.
- Discrimination or harassment.
- Criminal activity.
- Failure to maintain requirements (insurance, inspection, etc.).
- Violation of these Terms.

### 11.2 Deactivation Process
- You will receive notice of deactivation (except for safety violations).
- You may request an appeal within 30 days.
- Appeals are reviewed by our safety team.
- Decisions are final.

### 11.3 Reinstatement
After deactivation, you may:
- Appeal the decision.
- Complete required training or corrective actions.
- Reapply after 6 months (if eligible).
- Permanent deactivations are not reversible.
