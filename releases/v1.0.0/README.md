# NIDAW v1.0.0 - Initial Release

**Release Date:** July 27, 2026  
**Release Type:** Major  
**Status:** ✅ Production Ready

## 🎉 Welcome to NIDAW v1.0.0

This is the inaugural release of NIDAW - the world's first AI-native mobility super-app integrating transportation, accommodation, nourishment, logistics, and business services into a single self-optimizing ecosystem.

## 🚀 What's Included

### Core Services
- ✅ **Nidus** - Ride hailing with AI-powered driver matching
- ✅ **Haven** - Hotel and accommodation booking
- ✅ **Vorax** - Food delivery with real-time tracking
- ✅ **Logix** - Freight and logistics coordination
- ✅ **Corpus** - Corporate travel and expense management

### Platform Features
- ✅ Multi-region deployment (NA-East, EU-West, APAC-South)
- ✅ Real-time WebSocket updates
- ✅ AI/ML model serving (TorchServe + Ray Serve)
- ✅ Event-driven architecture (Apache Kafka)
- ✅ Blockchain integration (NIDAW Token)
- ✅ Autonomous vehicle support (experimental)

### Client Applications
- ✅ Flutter Super-App (iOS, Android, Web)
- ✅ Driver Mobile App
- ✅ Corporate Web Portal
- ✅ Partner Dashboard
